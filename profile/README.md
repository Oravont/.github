# Oravont Systems LLP

Defence R&D practice at the intersection of passive-sonar signal processing, underwater acoustic stealth and deep learning for underwater domain awareness.

We design submarine and ship platforms to keep their signatures quiet, and we build deep-learning systems that detect, classify and re-identify other vessels from the signatures they cannot avoid radiating.
